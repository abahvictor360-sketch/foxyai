## 5. Behance / Dribbble / Portfolio project description

**Title:** FoxyAI: AI Consulting Landing Page | Liquid-Glass UI & Motion Design

**Summary:**
FoxyAI is a landing page concept and build for an AI consultancy that "redesigns processes, connects data and deploys intelligent systems your team can actually use." The brief was to avoid the usual dark, neon AI look and create something warm, premium and human, while still feeling clearly technical.

**My role:** UI/UX design · Front-end development · Motion design · Image editing

**Highlights:**
- Liquid-glass design system: frosted panels with SVG refraction, highlight edges and a shine that tracks the cursor
- Code-drawn visuals: a neural network that reacts to the cursor, a perforated sphere and animated dunes
- Motion: staggered word reveals, 3D tilt cards, parallax layers, magnetic buttons, a ticker and a scroll progress bar
- AI-assisted image work: background removal on the team portraits and colour regrading of the hero robot to the brand orange
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile; reduced-motion support for accessibility; WebP images; deployed on Vercel

**Tools:** HTML · CSS · JavaScript · Canvas · Space Grotesk / JetBrains Mono · Vercel

**Tags (Behance/Dribbble):** landing page, web design, UI design, UX design, AI website, SaaS, glassmorphism, liquid glass, motion design, interaction design, responsive design, front-end, orange, startup, consulting

## Posting tips
1. **Video first.** Upload the MP4 directly to each platform instead of posting a link, because native video gets more reach. Then put the live link in the first comment on LinkedIn and in your bio on Instagram.
2. **Best times:** LinkedIn on Tue–Thu, 8–10am in your clients' time zone. Instagram and TikTok around 6–9pm.
3. **Hook in the first line.** It's the only text shown before "…see more".
4. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** This strongly boosts reach.
5. **Pin the post** to your LinkedIn Featured section and your Instagram grid.
6. **Reuse the content:** cut 3 short clips (hero, team tilt, cases) as separate Reels or Shorts over the next week.
